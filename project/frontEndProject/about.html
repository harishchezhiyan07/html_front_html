Our Dry Cleaning Process

Every garment goes through a professional 5-step cleaning process to ensure premium quality and customer satisfaction.

1

Inspection & Tagging

Your garments are collected and inspected for stains, missing buttons, tears, and other issues. Each item is tagged before being sent for sorting and processing.

2

Pre-Spotting

Tough stains are treated using eco-friendly cleaning solutions. Vacuum cleaning and gentle heat treatment prepare garments for the dry cleaning process.

3

Dry Cleaning

Clothes are cleaned using advanced non-water-based solvents to remove dirt and stains while protecting the fabric, leaving garments fresh and completely dry.

4

Post Spotting

Every garment is carefully inspected again. If any stain remains, it undergoes another round of pre-spotting and dry cleaning to achieve the best possible result.

5

Packaging & Delivery

After steam ironing, your garments are neatly folded or hung, securely packed, and delivered back to your preferred location, fresh, clean, and ready to wear.
